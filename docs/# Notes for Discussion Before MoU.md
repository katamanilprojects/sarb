# Notes for Discussion Before MoU

Academic Attendance & CIA Management Software

## 1. Background of the Project

1. Initially developed the software for my personal use to reduce manual academic workload.
2. Started with digitalisation of attendance records maintained in the Blue Book / Academic Record Book.
3. Gradually added:
   - Continuous Internal Assessment (CIA).
   - Student attendance viewing.
   - Access for other faculty members.
   - Extension to other departments.
4. As the software is now being used by multiple departments, the Principal suggested formalising its usage through an MoU.

## 2. Proposed MoU Duration

1. Initial MoU for 2 years.
2. Extendable for another 2 years with mutual consent.
3. Further extensions possible on mutually agreed terms, potentially throughout my tenure at the college.
4. After retirement, transfer, or leaving the institution, continued usage may be arranged through a fresh agreement.

## 3. Three Main Components of the MoU

1. Software Project — Ownership, usage rights, maintenance, upgrades, and enhancements.
2. Domain Name — Ownership, renewal, and DNS management.
3. Hosting Space — Hosting expenditure, server access, and technical responsibilities.

## 4. Software Project

### 4.1. Ownership

1. Software developed independently by me.
2. Complete ownership and intellectual property rights remain with me, subject to applicable institutional rules.
3. The college receives usage rights, not ownership.
4. I retain the right to use, modify, distribute, license, or commercialise the software for other institutions.
5. No transfer of source code ownership to the college.

### 4.2. Free Usage Rights

1. College can use the software completely free of charge throughout the MoU period.
2. No licence fees.
3. No subscription fees.
4. No software usage charges.
5. Authorised faculty, students, and administrators can use the software.

### 4.3. Maintenance and Upgrades

1. All routine maintenance is free.
2. Bug fixes and error corrections are free.
3. Performance improvements and upgrades are free.
4. Updates required due to changes in academic or administrative procedures are free.

### 4.4. New Requirements and Enhancements

1. All technically feasible new requirements requested by the college will be implemented free of charge.
2. Implementation timelines will depend on complexity, technical feasibility, and my academic responsibilities.
3. No additional development charges during the MoU period.
4. Any paid third-party services or additional infrastructure costs must be approved and arranged by the college.

## 5. Domain Name

### 5.1. Current Domain Arrangement

Main Domain: classattendance.in — owned and maintained by me.

Subdomains:

1. `ekr.classattendance.in` — Initially used for my personal academic work.
2. `jntuacea.classattendance.in` — Faculty / department portal.
3. `jntuaceastudents.classattendance.in` — Student portal.

### 5.2. Option A — Continue Existing Domain

1. College continues using the existing subdomains.
2. I will be responsible for renewal of `classattendance.in`.
3. I will maintain the required subdomains.
4. I will ensure proper DNS configuration and pointing to the hosting server.
5. No domain-related charges to the college during the MoU period.

### 5.3. Option B — College Uses Its Own Domain

1. College may choose its own domain or subdomains.
2. College will be responsible for:
   - Domain registration, if required.
   - Domain renewal charges.
   - DNS management.
   - Pointing the domains to the hosting server.
3. I will provide necessary technical guidance and software-side configuration.

Decision required: Continue with existing subdomains or move to college-controlled domains.

## 6. Hosting Space

### 6.1. Current Hosting Arrangement

1. Software is presently hosted on Hostinger.
2. Hostinger account is maintained by University SDC.
3. Current plan: Hostinger Business Web Hosting.
4. Existing plan is sufficient for present software requirements.
5. Approximate promotional cost: Up to ₹15,000 for 4 years, subject to actual pricing, taxes, and renewal rates.

### 6.2. Hosting Responsibility

1. College will be responsible for arranging hosting during the MoU period.
2. College / University SDC will take care of hosting renewal.
3. Hosting expenditure will be borne or arranged by the college.
4. If additional hosting resources are required in the future, the same will be discussed with the college.
5. Hosting charges are separate from the free software services.

### 6.3. Current Hosting Access

1. Hostinger account is controlled by SDC.
2. Account is protected through two-factor authentication.
3. We do not have direct Hostinger control-panel access.
4. Currently, limited FTP access is available for managing files in the relevant project folder.
5. FTP access is sufficient for routine file updates.

### 6.4. Additional Access Required

For certain maintenance activities, access may be needed to:

1. Hosting control panel.
2. Server error logs.
3. Performance and configuration settings.
4. Database management facilities.
5. Other technical settings required for troubleshooting or upgrades.

Requirement: SDC should provide necessary access or timely technical assistance whenever required, preferably through secure, restricted-access arrangements.

### 6.5. Hosting-Related Responsibilities

1. I will handle application-level software issues.
2. College / SDC will handle hosting subscription and infrastructure administration.
3. College / SDC should assist with server-related technical issues.
4. Software maintenance should not be held responsible for interruptions caused solely by hosting failures, expired subscriptions, or unavailable administrative access.

## 7. Student and Institutional Data

### 7.1. Data Ownership

1. Student attendance, CIA marks, and other official academic records belong to the college.
2. Software ownership remains with me.
3. Institutional data and software intellectual property are separate.

### 7.2. Data Security and Backups

1. Application-level security will be maintained by me.
2. Hosting-level security will be managed by the college / SDC.
3. Database backup arrangements must be established.
4. College / SDC should ensure backup storage and recovery facilities.
5. I will provide necessary software-level technical assistance for backup and restoration.
6. Institutional data will not be used for external commercial purposes without appropriate authorisation.

## 8. Future Development and Commercial Usage

1. I retain complete rights to continue developing the software independently.
2. I may provide the same software to other colleges or institutions.
3. I may offer the software commercially.
4. No permission from the college is required for independent use or commercialisation, subject to applicable law and existing obligations.
5. College-specific confidential information and student data will not be shared with other institutions.
6. Enhancements developed during the MoU will remain part of my software, unless separately agreed otherwise.

## 9. Retirement, Transfer or Leaving the College

1. The MoU is initially valid for 2 years, irrespective of employment changes, unless otherwise agreed.
2. In case of retirement, transfer, or leaving the college, the parties will discuss continuation arrangements.
3. Continued software usage and maintenance after the existing agreement expires will require a fresh agreement or written extension.
4. College data will remain accessible for authorised export and transition.
5. Software ownership will continue to remain with me.

## 10. MoU Expiry or Termination

1. Either party may terminate the MoU with 90 days' written notice, subject to mutual agreement on this condition.
2. Before termination, institutional data should be made available for export.
3. Necessary technical assistance may be provided for data migration.
4. Continued software usage after expiry requires a new agreement or written authorisation.
5. Domain and hosting transition arrangements should be finalised before discontinuation.
6. Software intellectual property rights remain with me.

## 11. Parties to the MoU

### First Party

Myself, in my individual capacity as the software developer and owner.

### Second Party

The college, represented by the Principal or other duly authorised signatory.

### Role of SDC

1. SDC is not a direct party to the MoU.
2. SDC presently maintains the hosting account.
3. College will coordinate with SDC regarding hosting, renewal, technical access, and infrastructure support.
4. Written concurrence from SDC may be obtained for hosting-related responsibilities.

Confirm free usage, maintenance, upgrades, and technically feasible enhancements.
