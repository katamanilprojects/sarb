# Student Academic Record Book

Faculty User Manual

Beginner guide for faculty members

Revised and checked 23 October 2026

Use this manual to manage your subjects, record classes, enter marks, review feedback, and prepare course reports. Each numbered feature tells you where to go, what to do, and how to check the result.

## Start here

1. Get the staff portal address, username, and password from your college.
2. Log in and change your initial password.
3. Check your subjects and weekly timetable. Ask your HOD to correct missing assignments.
4. Before teaching, check Course Outcomes and prepare your lesson plan.
5. After each class, save attendance and the teaching diary.
6. After each assessment, enter marks, review them, and complete final submission.
7. At semester end, check attainment, reconcile course delivery, and submit your course completion report.

## How to follow the instructions

- **Bold text** names a menu, button, or field on the screen.
- A path such as **Other Options > Enter CIA Marks & MetaData** tells you what to click, in order.
- Always check the subject code, class or section, and academic year before entering data.
- A success message confirms an action. Reopen the relevant record to check the saved details.
- Some features depend on college settings. A missing option or a disabled button may require help from your HOD or portal administrator.
- On a phone or a small screen, scroll sideways inside wide student or marks tables to see every column.

Use the maximum marks, available dates, and access messages shown for your selected subject. The college controls which features are available to your account.

## Common words

- **HOD:** Head of Department.
- **CO:** Course Outcome, a statement of what students should learn in a subject.
- **PO / PSO:** Program Outcome / Program Specific Outcome.
- **CIA:** Continuous Internal Assessment, such as internal tests and assignments.
- **SEE:** Semester End Examination.
- **OBE:** Outcome Based Education, which checks whether learning outcomes are achieved.
- **CQI / ATR:** Continuous Quality Improvement / Action Taken Report.
- **CSV:** A spreadsheet saved as comma separated values for upload. An Excel workbook ending in .xlsx is a different file type.
- **QP metadata:** Question paper details, including question marks, Bloom levels, and CO mappings.
- **Attainment:** A measure of how well students achieved the learning outcomes.
- **Articulation matrix:** A table linking COs to the program's POs and PSOs.
- **Reconciliation:** Matching actual teaching diary entries to planned lectures.
- **Pedagogy:** The teaching method, such as a discussion, demonstration, or lecture.

## Understand the main menu

- **Home:** Read portal guidance and open your weekly timetable or learning resources.
- **Mark Attendance:** Record student attendance and the topics taught.
- **Show Attendance:** View attendance, diary entries, CIA records, and related downloads.
- **Other Options:** Open course planning, assessments, feedback, and attendance utilities.
- **Change Password:** Set a new password for your account.
- **Logout:** End your session after saving your work.

## Save or submit

**Save**, **Save Draft**, and **Save without Submitting** keep work at the stage stated on that screen. **Submit Final Marks**, **Finalize reviewed marks**, and **Submit Course Completion to HOD** complete a further submission or review step. Always check the resulting status.

## Account

### 1 Log in to the faculty portal

**Where:** Staff portal login page.

1. Open the staff portal address supplied by your college.
2. Enter your username and password exactly as provided.
3. Click **Login**.
4. Check that the faculty **Home** page opens under your account.

**Check:** Your name and the faculty menu appear on **Home**.

**If login is locked:** Read the waiting time shown. The current login page locks further attempts for 15 minutes after 5 failed attempts.

### 2 Change your password

**Where:** **Change Password** in the main menu.

1. Enter your current password in **Enter Existing Password**.
2. Enter a password different from the current one in **Enter New Password**.
3. Enter it again in **Confirm New Password**.
4. Click **Update** and read the message.
5. If successful, use the new password the next time you log in.

**Help:** If you cannot remember the current password, contact the college staff who manage faculty accounts for a reset.

### 3 Log out safely

**Where:** **Logout** in the main menu.

1. Save the work you are entering and check the success message.
2. Click **Logout**.
3. Check that you return to the login page.
4. Close the browser tab when using a shared computer.

## Mark class attendance

### 5 Save attendance and the teaching diary

**Where:** **Mark Attendance** in the main menu.

**Before you start:** Your subject and students must be assigned. Keep the class date, periods, and list of present students ready.

1. Select the **Subject**. Check its code, class, section, and academic year.
2. Select the class **Date** within the allowed course dates. Future attendance dates are not accepted.
3. Click **Get Students**.
4. Enter the topics covered in **Teaching Diary**.
5. Select the correct **Period / Hours** from the available list.
6. Tick the students who were present. Leave absent students unticked.
7. If most students were present, use **Select/Unselect All Students**, then untick those who were absent.
8. Check the displayed number of students present and tick the confirmation checkbox.
9. Click **Save Attendance** once and wait for the result.
10. Open **Show Attendance** and verify the date, periods, attendance, and diary.

**Check:** The success message says attendance was marked. The saved session appears in the attendance record.

**Example:** If 28 of 30 students attended, tick those 28 students. Confirm that the displayed count is 28 before saving.

**For more than one period:** Select multiple periods only when the same attendance and diary apply to all of them. If the students or topics differ, save the periods separately.

**If an hour is missing:** It may already be recorded or unavailable for that date. Check **Show Attendance** before trying another date or period.

**If a student is missing:** Ask the HOD to check subject enrollment. Do not select another student's name as a substitute.

**If the connection fails:** Reopen **Show Attendance** to check whether the session saved before submitting again.

## Correct student attendance

### 6 Change one student from present to absent or absent to present

**Where:** **Other Options > Update Single Student Attendance**.

**Before you start:** Use this for an existing attendance entry. The date must be today or no more than 7 days earlier.

1. Select the **Subject** and **Date**.
2. Click **Get Hours**.
3. Select the **Hour**, then click **Get Students**.
4. Select the **Student**, then click **Get Attendance**.
5. Check the student's current status.
6. Choose **Present** or **Absent** in **Change Status To**.
7. Enter a clear **Reason for correction**.
8. Click **Update Attendance**.
9. Verify the changed status in **Show Attendance**.

**Example reason:** "Student attended period 2 but was accidentally marked absent."

**If the date is too old:** Contact your HOD or portal administrator. The faculty correction screen will not accept a date outside its allowed window.

### 7 Add a missing student to an already recorded session

**Where:** **Other Options > Mark Attendance (For Exceptional Cases)**.

**Use when:** A student has no attendance entry for a session that is already recorded, for example after a late enrollment is added.

1. Select the **Subject** and **Date**.
2. Click **Get Marked Hours**.
3. Select the recorded **Hour**.
4. Click **Get Unmarked Students**.
5. Tick only the listed students whose presence you can confirm.
6. Check the selected count and tick the confirmation checkbox.
7. Click **Mark Selected Students as Present**.
8. Verify the new entry in **Show Attendance**.

**Check:** This adds present entries for selected students who had no record. To change an existing absent entry, use feature 6.

## Session deletion and diary only

### 8 Request deletion of a wrong session

**Where:** **Other Options > Submit New Deletion Request**.

**Use when:** The whole attendance session was entered for the wrong date, subject, or period. The HOD reviews the request.

1. Select the **Subject** and **Date**, then click **Get Hours**.
2. Select the wrong **Hour**, then click **Get Details**.
3. Check the displayed session carefully.
4. Enter a specific **Reason for Delete**.
5. Click **Submit Request**.
6. Track the decision using feature 9. If approved, check **Show Attendance** before entering any replacement session.

**Check:** Sending a request does not immediately delete the session. Approval removes the affected attendance and diary entries.

### 9 Track a deletion request

**Where:** **Other Options > View Submitted Requests**.

1. Open the request list.
2. Find the subject, class date, and hour you requested.
3. Read the status: **Pending**, **Approved**, or **Rejected**.
4. For a pending request, allow the HOD to review it. For a rejected request, discuss the correction with the HOD.
5. For an approved request, verify that the intended session was removed.

### 10 Record a diary entry without student attendance

**Where:** **Other Options > Add Diary Only (No Attendance)**.

1. Select the **Subject** and **Date**.
2. Click **Get Timings**.
3. Enter the diary text and select the relevant period or periods.
4. Tick **I accept, Only Diary is Added Without Student Attendance**.
5. Click **Save Dairy**. This is the button spelling in the portal.
6. Open **Show Attendance > Show Diary** and check the entry.

**Check:** This records only the diary. It does not mark students present or absent. Follow your college's practice for when to use it.

## Attendance records and downloads

### 11 View overall attendance

**Where:** **Show Attendance** in the main menu.

1. Select the **Subject**.
2. To view the full available period, leave **Select Date Range? : No** selected.
3. To limit the report, choose **Yes** and enter **Start Date** and **End Date**.
4. Click **View Overall Attendance**.
5. Review each student's **Overall Percentage** and the attendance shortage lists below it. Use the detailed view in feature 12 for class counts.

### 12 View detailed attendance and download the e Bluebook

**Where:** **Show Attendance**.

1. Select the subject and, if needed, the date range.
2. Click **Detailed Attendance & e-Bluebook**.
3. Review the date and period entries for the students.
4. Click **Download Detailed Attendance** for a CSV report, or **Download e-Bluebook PDF** for the academic record book, where enabled.
5. Open the downloaded file and check the subject and date coverage.

**Check:** The detailed attendance download uses your selected dates. The e-Bluebook also contains course information beyond the attendance pages; check each section's coverage.

### 13 Read the saved teaching diary

**Where:** **Show Attendance**.

1. Select the subject and date range if required.
2. Click **Show Diary**.
3. Check the dates, periods, and topics covered.
4. Use these records when matching teaching to your lesson plan in feature 47.

### 14 View CIA records and export the marks sheet

**Where:** **Show Attendance**.

1. Select the subject.
2. Click **Show Continuous Internal Assessment**.
3. Review the available assessment marks and attachments.
4. Click **Download as Excel** where shown to save the displayed marks report.
5. Open the file and check student names, assessments, and totals.

**Date range:** Attendance and diary records use the date filter; CIA records use assessment data. For an empty report, check filters and final marks submission.

## Course Outcomes

### 15 Import approved Course Outcomes

**Where:** **Other Options > Add / View Course Outcomes (COs)**.

1. Select the subject and click **Get COs**.
2. If approved curriculum outcomes are available, review the import option and any existing outcomes.
3. Click **Import Course Outcomes & Matrix** when offered. If the screen says **Inherited from Curriculum Master Catalog**, use **Import as Editable Subject COs** when you need subject copies.
4. Read the result message.
5. Check the imported CO statements and any imported matrix mappings.

**Before importing:** Coordinate with your course team. Outcomes and matrix mappings may synchronize with the curriculum master and parallel sections.

### 16 Add Course Outcomes manually

**Where:** The same Course Outcomes screen.

1. Select the subject and click **Get COs**.
2. If no outcomes are listed, enter how many COs the approved syllabus defines and click **Generate Fields**.
3. For each CO, enter its statement, choose its **Bloom's Taxonomy Level (BTL)**, and enter the approved **Target Threshold Limit (%)**.
4. Click **Save All COs**.
5. To add a missing CO later, click **Click here to Add New/Missing CO**.
6. Check the automatically assigned **CO Number**. Enter the statement, Bloom level, and threshold, then click **Add CO**.
7. Check that every required CO is listed once.

**Example statement:** "Write SQL queries to retrieve information from a database."

### 17 Edit an existing Course Outcome

**Where:** The same Course Outcomes screen.

1. Load the subject's COs.
2. Click the edit button beside the relevant CO.
3. Correct the statement, Bloom level, or threshold as permitted.
4. Click **Update CO** followed by the CO number, such as **Update CO1**.
5. Reload the CO list and check the change.

**Check:** Follow the approved syllabus and department targets. If fields are locked or a change affects other sections, ask your course coordinator or HOD before proceeding.

## Outcome mapping and lesson planning

### 18 Map Course Outcomes to program outcomes

**Where:** **Other Options > Add / View CO-PO/PSO Articulation Matrix**.

**Before you start:** COs and department PO / PSO definitions must be available.

1. Select the subject.
2. Click **Add / View Matrix**.
3. Read the CO rows and PO / PSO columns.
4. Where entry is available, type a strength for each relevant mapping: **1 = Low**, **2 = Medium**, or **3 = High**.
5. Leave unrelated mapping cells blank.
6. Click **Add Mappings** and confirm the prompt.
7. Reload the matrix and check the saved values.

**Check:** Existing mappings may open for viewing only. Coordinate corrections with the HOD. The portal can synchronize mappings with the master curriculum and other sections.

### 19 Add a lecture to your lesson plan

**Where:** **Other Options > Add / View Lesson Plan (Course Delivery Plan)**.

**Before you start:** Check the subject's COs and keep the syllabus ready.

1. Select the course and click **Load Plan**.
2. Enter **Unit #** and a unique **Lecture #**.
3. Select the **Target Course Outcome**.
4. Enter the **Planned Syllabus Topic / Concept**.
5. Choose the **Bloom's Level** and **Teaching Pedagogy**.
6. Enter **Reference Material** if needed.
7. Click **Save Lecture Plan**.
8. Check the lecture in the plan table. Repeat for the remaining lectures.

**Example:** Unit 1, Lecture 1, topic "Introduction to database systems," linked to the relevant CO, with the appropriate teaching method.

**Check:** A lesson plan describes what you intend to teach. Record the actual topics taught in the diary when you conduct each class.

## Import and maintain the lesson plan

### 20 Upload a full lesson plan from a spreadsheet

**Where:** The lesson plan screen, under **Bulk Excel / CSV Import**.

1. Load the correct subject's plan.
2. Click **Download CSV Template**.
3. Open the template in Excel or another spreadsheet application.
4. Replace the example rows with your actual lecture plan. Keep the existing column order and headings. Use unique lecture numbers and valid CO numbers for this subject.
5. Save the completed file as **CSV**, keeping the template headings unchanged.
6. Choose the file under **Upload Completed CSV**.
7. Click **Upload & Populate Plan**.
8. Read the imported or updated count and any skipped-row message. Check the lecture numbers, topics, COs, Bloom levels, and planned hours in the resulting table.

**Check:** A row with an existing lecture number updates that lecture. Upload can save valid rows while skipping others. Review the saved plan before correcting and uploading again.

### 21 Edit or delete one planned lecture

**Where:** The lesson plan table.

1. Select the subject and click **Load Plan**.
2. Find the lecture by its number and topic.
3. To edit it, click the **Edit Lecture** icon, change the fields, and click **Save Lecture Plan**.
4. To remove it, click the **Delete Lecture** icon and confirm when prompted.
5. Check the updated table and lecture count.

### 22 Clear the whole lesson plan

**Where:** The lesson plan screen > **Clear All Lectures**.

1. Confirm the subject and review the full plan.
2. Keep a copy of the plan you need to retain before clearing it.
3. Click **Clear All Lectures**.
4. Read and accept the confirmation only if you intend to remove the entire plan.
5. Check the result before adding or importing the replacement plan.

**If editing is locked:** A submitted or approved course completion audit can lock the lesson plan. Ask the HOD to review or return the submission when a correction is needed.

## Begin CIA entry and question details

### 23 Open the correct internal assessment

**Where:** **Other Options > Enter CIA Marks & MetaData**.

1. Select the **Subject**.
2. Click **Get Continuous Assessment Status**.
3. Find the required assessment, such as CIA 1 or CIA 2, or the component shown for your course type.
4. Check whether its status is **Not Added** or **Added**.
5. Choose **Add/View Detailed** to enter question or activity marks, **Add Overall** or **Add** for the available totals form, or **Attachments** to manage supporting files.

**Choose your workflow:** Use features 24 to 29 for theory, features 30 to 31 for laboratory, feature 32 for Design Thinking, feature 33 for PG, and feature 34 for projects.

### 24 Add question paper details and CO mappings

**Where:** The selected CIA assessment's **Add/View Detailed** page.

1. Check that COs are already defined. Find the relevant component, such as Subjective, Objective, or Assignment.
2. Click **Add Questions**. Select the required template if a template list appears; the page loads the next form automatically.
3. For Subjective or Internal Exam components, choose **Single**, **a & b**, or **a & b & c** for each question, then click **Next: Add Question MetaData**.
4. Objective, Assignment, or Activity components may open the metadata table directly. Continue with the table shown.
5. Check each question's label and marks. Set editable marks, choose the question or activity type and Bloom level, and tick at least one relevant CO for every row.
6. Click **Save Question MetaData**.
7. Review **View Questions**, then use **Next Step: Marks Entry** when ready.

**To add another assignment:** Return to the component list, click **Click here Add New Assignment**, and configure its questions and marks.

**Check:** The metadata must match the actual assessment paper. Maximum marks depend on the selected subject and regulation.

**Before saving:** Check the total marks of all subparts. The parts of one question must add up to that question's intended maximum.

## Detailed CIA marks

### 25 Enter marks question by question

**Where:** CIA component list > **Marks Entry**, or **View Questions > Next Step: Marks Entry**.

1. Check the subject, CIA number, and component name.
2. Match each student using the admission number and name.
3. Enter every required score within the displayed question maximum. The current detailed CIA grid uses increments of 0.5 marks.
4. Review the entries against your evaluation record.
5. Click **Save Marks**.
6. Check the saved **View Marks** table.
7. Repeat for the other components before submitting overall CIA marks in feature 28.

### 26 Upload question marks using CSV

**Where:** The selected component's **Marks Entry** screen.

**Before you start:** CSV tools appear for initial entry. If this component already has student scores and opens **View Marks**, use **Edit Marks** for corrections.

1. Click **Download CSV Template**.
2. Open the file and retain its student identifiers and question headings exactly.
3. Enter the evaluated marks within each question's maximum. Check every required cell.
4. Save as CSV and choose the file on the portal.
5. Click **Upload CSV**.
6. Check the saved marks for every student and question. Correct missing or incorrect entries before final submission.

**Check:** An upload message alone is not enough. Review the resulting table, especially values near the maximum and any cells that were blank in your file.

**CIA blanks:** This import can treat a blank mark as zero. Upload only verified scores and enter zero explicitly for an assessed zero. Use PG draft rules in feature 33 for unassessed PG scores.

## Correct CIA details before submission

### 27 Correct marks or question details before final submission

**Where:** The CIA component's **View Marks** or **View Questions** page.

**To correct individual scores:**

1. Open the component's **View Marks** page.
2. Click **Edit Marks**.
3. Correct the student's scores and click **Save Marks**.
4. Review the saved table and return using **CIA Components**.

**To remove all student scores for one component:**

1. Open **View Marks** and confirm the component name.
2. Click **Delete Marks** and read the confirmation. It removes all student scores for that component.
3. Confirm only if you intend to re-enter those scores.
4. Open **Marks Entry**, enter or upload the replacement scores, and check them again.

**To rebuild incorrect question metadata:**

1. Open **View Questions** for the correct component.
2. Click **Delete Meta Data** and read the warning in the confirmation window.
3. Confirm the deletion only after keeping any evaluation record you need. Linked question scores can be removed.
4. Return to the component list and use **Add Questions** to recreate the correct structure and CO mappings.
5. Re-enter the component's student scores and review them before final CIA submission.

**If controls are unavailable:** Final submission or college permissions may lock changes. Contact your HOD or portal administrator for the permitted correction process.

## Review and submit CIA totals

### 28 Submit CIA totals calculated from detailed marks

**Where:** CIA component list > **View & Submit CIA Marks**.

**Before you start:** Save detailed marks for all required components.

1. Open the assessment's **Add/View Detailed** page.
2. Check that the required components and marks are complete.
3. Click **View & Submit CIA Marks**.
4. Review each student's component totals and overall total.
5. If a value is wrong, return to the relevant detailed marks screen and correct it.
6. When the totals are correct, click **Submit Final Marks**. The theory, lab, and Design Thinking review pages use this label.
7. Return to the assessment status page, select the subject again if needed, and check the submitted record.

**Check:** Saving detailed marks and submitting final CIA marks are separate actions. Use **View Submitted CIA Marks** to check the finalized record where shown.

### 29 Enter overall theory marks directly

**Where:** CIA assessment status > **Add Overall**, where available.

**Use when:** Your college asks for overall component marks and you have the verified totals ready. Question details are needed for question-based analysis.

1. Open the selected theory assessment's overall entry form.
2. Enter the student's Subjective, Objective, and Assignment marks in the matching columns.
3. Keep every value within the maximum printed on the screen.
4. To return later, click **Save without Submitting**.
5. When all entries are complete and checked, click **Validate & Submit Marks**.
6. Return to the assessment status page, select the subject again if needed, and open **View Overall** or **View**.
7. Check the submitted student marks and totals.

**Remember:** A temporary save is a draft. Use the displayed assessment rules; do not assume that all courses share the same weighting or maximum.

## Laboratory assessment

### 30 Enter laboratory totals and use the rubrics assistant

**Where:** CIA assessment status > laboratory subject > **Add** under Overall.

1. Open the laboratory assessment and check its displayed maximum marks.
2. Enter each student's day-to-day assessment mark and internal test mark.
3. If rubrics are enabled, click the calculator button beside that student's **Day-to-Day** score.
4. Enter the score for each configured criterion within its limit.
5. Click **Apply to Student Score**.
6. Check the resulting day-to-day total and the internal test mark in the main form.
7. Click **Save without Submitting** to continue later, or **Validate & Submit Marks** when complete.
8. Open the submitted laboratory marks and verify them.

**Check:** Applying a rubric fills the student's score in the form. Complete the main form's save or submit action as well.

**Drafts:** Laboratory draft saving still requires valid numeric scores in both columns for all listed students. Keep the rubric breakdown for final submission; a draft saves the score totals.

**Condensed summary:** To review scaled day-to-day and internal exam marks calculated according to statutory limits, open the condensed laboratory marks register (**facuglabciamarkscondensed.php**).

## Laboratory marks by date

### 31 Record laboratory marks by date and in bulk

**Where:** Laboratory CIA > **Add/View Detailed**.

1. Click **Click here to Add New Day-to-Day Component**.
2. Select the activity date and click **Confirm Add Day-to-Day Component**.
3. Check the new date row in the component list. The portal creates the date question automatically. Use that row's **Marks Entry** to enter scores for one date.
4. For several dates, open **Bulk Day-to-Day Marks Entry**.
5. Select the date columns you want to enter, fill every required student score within the shown maximum, and click **Save Selected Marks**. Dates marked **already entered** are skipped by this bulk tool.
6. Alternatively, download the CSV template from this screen, fill it without changing identifiers or headings, and use **Upload CSV**.
7. Review the saved marks for all selected dates.
8. Enter the **Internal Exam** component's question details and student marks as well. Then open **View & Submit CIA Marks**, check both totals, and click **Submit Final Marks**.

**Choose the required method:** Follow the college's laboratory assessment process. Check existing marks before using another entry method for the same assessment.

**To enter one date only:** Use that date component's **Marks Entry** and **Save Marks**. Correct existing date scores through **View Marks > Edit Marks**, before final submission.

**If the bulk link is missing:** **Bulk Day-to-Day Marks Entry** appears after the course end date when unentered date components remain. During the course, use each date's **Marks Entry**. Use the bulk tool for initial entry; it skips dates that already contain scores.

3. Review the calculated total for each student.
4. Click **Save without Submitting** for a draft or **Validate & Submit Marks** for the completed assessment.
5. Open **View** from the assessment status page and check the submitted totals.

## Assessment attachments

### 35 Upload and manage CIA supporting files

**Where:** CIA assessment status > **Attachments** for the correct assessment.

**Use for:** Question papers, evaluation records, and other assessment evidence requested by the college.

1. Check the subject and assessment number at the top of the page.
2. Click **Add New Attachment**.
3. Enter a clear **Title of the File**, such as "CIA 1 Question Paper".
4. Choose the file under **Attachment**.
5. Click **Submit**.
6. Check the file title in the attachment list.
7. Click **Download** and open the file to confirm it is the correct document.

**File limits:** Use .pdf, .doc, .docx, .jpg, .png, .xls, .xlsx, or .csv. Keep each file below 20 MB; your college's server may impose a lower upload limit.

**Attachment limit:** The screen allows up to 5 attachments per assessment. If the limit is reached, review existing files before adding another.

**To replace an incorrect file:** Download a copy if needed, use the **X** delete control beside the correct file, and then upload the replacement. Check the title and contents again.

**To download marks evidence:** Use the component **Download** links where available on this page to get the marks CSV files. To view the complete component marks register question by question, open **View Marks** (**facviewmarks.php**); use **Export to CSV** (**facviewmarkscsv.php**) to download an offline spreadsheet copy.

**To use the supplied formats:** Open the reference links for the sample evaluation record in Excel and the sample question paper in Word. Fill your own copy and upload it to the correct assessment when required.

**If upload or deletion is disabled:** The metadata module may be read only, or the attachment limit may have been reached. Check the message and contact the portal administrator if access is needed.

## Common problems and who can help

### My subject or students are missing

1. Check the academic year, class, section, and subject code.
2. Ask the HOD to verify your subject assignment and student enrollment.
3. Reopen the screen after the mapping is corrected.

### A menu is missing or saving is disabled

1. Check for a read-only or feature availability message.
2. Check whether the assessment or course file was already submitted.
3. Contact the HOD or portal administrator with the page name and the exact message.

### A form asks me to refresh or log in again

1. Keep a private note of unsaved work if needed.
2. Reopen the page or log in again.
3. Check existing records before re-entering or resubmitting data.

### A CSV upload does not give the expected result

1. Download a fresh template for the exact course and component.
2. Keep its headings and student identifiers unchanged.
3. Check marks, required fields, and the CSV file type.
4. Read the upload messages and verify the saved rows before final submission.

### Marks or attainment look incomplete

1. Check the selected course and assessment.
2. Verify detailed marks, final submission, question mappings, and feedback response counts.
3. Ask the academic section about missing assessment rules or published result notifications.

### A file does not download

1. Check your browser's downloads list and allow the portal's download or new tab if prompted.
2. Check that the feature is enabled and your login is still active.
3. Try the download again. If it fails, share the exact message with the portal administrator.

**When asking for help:** Include the subject code, class or section, academic year, assessment or session date and hour, the action you tried, and the error text. Share only the records needed through your college's approved support channel.
